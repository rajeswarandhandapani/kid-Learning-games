For grown-ups

Your child chooses an age on the first visit, and the games follow it. Ages are a guide, not a limit: tap the age at the top of the page, or Easier and Harder under a subject's games, to change it. See and search all 84 games.

Learning without pressure

Every game starts in untimed practice. Children can read feedback and move on when ready. A timed challenge is optional, under More ways to play.

Progress & privacy

Stars, favorites, and achievements are saved in this browser. They are not synced between devices. Clearing browser data removes them.

Use it like an app, even offline

Every game, picture and Tamil recording is saved on this device the first time you visit, so the games keep working without internet.
