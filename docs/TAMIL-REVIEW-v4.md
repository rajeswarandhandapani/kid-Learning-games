**What to look for:** spelling, natural wording for a child of 5 to 9, and whether the English matches the Tamil.

## 3. The new word order game, சொல் வரிசை

The child puts the words of a sentence in order, using the sentences with three or more words from sections 1 and 4.

**Please decide:** Tamil word order is flexible, and the game accepts only the order written here. So a child who writes "தண்ணீர் நாம் குடிக்கிறோம்" for "நாம் தண்ணீர் குடிக்கிறோம்" is told to try again, and then shown the written sentence as "one good sentence" (ஒரு நல்ல வாக்கியம்). The game's hint also says "In Tamil, the doing word usually comes last." If that feels wrong, this game can be changed or removed.
